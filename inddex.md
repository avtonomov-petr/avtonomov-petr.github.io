# Petr Avtonomov

**Independent researcher — inverse problems, regularization, and the audit of formal methods.**

Physicist by training. I work on the boundary between a mathematical method and
the domain in which it is applied: where does the algorithm stop measuring the
object and start measuring its own preprocessing? My work develops a *regularization
audit* — a procedure for separating formal stability from substantive validity —
and applies it across engineering, chemometrics, and the digital humanities.

## Selected work

**Engineering systems — failure modes of inverse-problem diagnostics.**
Seven technical notes on dam seepage, volcanic tremor, network flows, structural
health monitoring, chaotic data assimilation, ultrasonic testing of composites, and
turbulent intermittency. Unified verdict: *a diagnostic is a detector, not an oracle*.
→ [Collection DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23854762)

**Digital humanities — calibration of stylometric pipelines.**
A calibration audit of blind SVD on the Federalist Papers (85 essays). Blind SVD
produces a stable component that explains 23% of variance and is dominated by the
article *the*; it does not separate Hamilton from Madison (p = 0.45). Calibration on
eleven function words recovers the authorial signal (p < 0.0001) and classifies
10 of 12 disputed papers in agreement with the historical consensus.
*(Preprint forthcoming.)*

## What I am interested in

- Independent validation and model-risk audit of inverse-problem pipelines.
- Reproducible research on regularization: code, data, specification curves.
- Collaboration with groups working on inverse problems in industry, heritage
  science, or computational humanities.
